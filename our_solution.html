About Our Model

A FULLY MANAGED VENDING SOLUTION

At Flash Innovations, we provide a simple and hassle-free vending machine solution designed for workplaces, hotels, colleges, warehouses, factories, and other high-traffic facilities.

Our goal is to help organisations offer convenient access to snacks and beverages while creating additional revenue and supporting meaningful community initiatives.

Unlike traditional vending operators, Flash Innovations manages the entire process from start to finish — we supply the machine, install it, stock it, maintain it, and service it regularly, allowing our partners to enjoy all the benefits without any of the work.

  • No upfront cost — machine, install & setup all free
  • Earn 15% monthly revenue from every sale
  • We handle all stocking, cleaning & servicing
  • Modern cashless payment technology included
  • 5% of sales donated to Sydney Children's Hospitals Foundation
