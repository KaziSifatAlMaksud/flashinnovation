ABOUT FLASH INNOVATIONS

We're on a mission to deliver premium, fully managed vending solutions while giving back to the communities we serve.

WHO WE ARE

Flash Innovations is a Sydney-based vending solutions company dedicated to making premium snack and beverage machines accessible to workplaces, hotels, educational institutions, and more — all with zero upfront cost.

Founded with a passion for seamless service and community impact, we handle everything from site assessment and installation to ongoing stocking, maintenance, and monthly reporting. Our partners simply enjoy the benefits.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To deliver fully managed, technology-forward vending solutions that create value for organisations, generate passive revenue, and contribute meaningfully to our community through charitable giving.

Our Vision

To be NSW's most trusted vending partner — known not only for premium service and modern machines, but for the positive difference we make in the lives of sick children and their families across the state.

OUR CHARITY COMMITMENT

Flash Innovations proudly donates 5% of all machine sales to the Sydney Children's Hospitals Foundation. This is not an afterthought — it's baked into our business model. Every snack sold, every drink purchased, contributes to the care of sick children and their families across New South Wales.

When you partner with us, you become part of something bigger. Your workplace or venue isn't just getting a vending machine — it's joining a movement of businesses that believe in doing good.
